What does this tool do?
The big picture — before you touch anything

The Outreach Builder is an AI-powered sales email tool. You tell it about your product, give it a list of companies to reach out to, and it writes a personalised sales email for each company — automatically. Then it tracks exactly who opened it, how long they read it, and whether they clicked.

Upload Your Product Document
The AI reads your Word doc and builds your "benefit toggles" automatically

This is where the magic starts. You give the tool a Word document that describes your product or service — things like features, benefits, pricing, use cases. The AI reads it and creates a set of "Benefit Toggles" that you can switch on or off for each email.

1
Prepare your Word document
Open Microsoft Word (or Google Docs → Download as .docx). Write paragraphs about your product: what it does, key benefits, who it's for, pricing if relevant. Plain paragraph text works best — no need for fancy formatting.
2
Find the "Upload Your Word Doc" section
Scroll down past the Campaign Tracker. You'll see a big dashed box labelled "Drop your Word doc here". This is Step 1 on the dashboard.
3
Click "Browse .docx file" and select your document
Or simply drag and drop the file directly onto the dashed box. The tool accepts .doc and .docx files only.
4
Wait ~10 seconds for the AI to process
You'll see a message: "AI is reading your document and building benefit toggles…". Don't close the browser. It will say ✅ Done! when finished.
5
Review your auto-generated toggles
You'll now see coloured buttons on the right side — these are your benefit toggles. Each one represents a key point from your document. Click the ✏️ pencil icon on any toggle to rename it or edit its content.

Now you tell the tool who to write emails for. Each "prospect" is a company you want to reach out to. The more info you give (industry, email address), the better the personalisation.

It's in the left column below the toggle upload section. You'll see input fields for company name, email, and industry.
2

Generate & Send Your Email
Select a prospect, choose your toggles, copy the email and send
1
Click a company in the Prospects list
The company row highlights in green. This selects it as the target for your email. All following steps apply to this company.
2
Activate the benefit toggles you want to include
Look at the mini toggle grid below the prospect list (labelled "Active toggles for email"). Click any toggle to switch it on (green) or off. Only the green ones will appear in the email. Choose 2–4 for best results — don't overwhelm the reader.
3
Click "🔄 Rebuild Email"
The AI will generate a personalised email for the selected company using your active toggles, their industry, and the tone you set. This takes 5–15 seconds. A ⏳ Generating… badge will show while it works.
4
Review the email in the Preview panel (Step 3)
The right-hand column shows the Email Preview. Read through it. If you want a different version, just click "🔄 Rebuild Email" again — the AI generates a fresh version each time.
5
Click "📋 Copy Email HTML"
This copies the full email (including the invisible read-time tracking pixel) to your clipboard. The email is formatted as HTML.
6
Paste into Gmail or Outlook and send
In Gmail: Compose a new email → click the three dots menu (⋮) at the bottom → select "Paste as HTML" or just press Ctrl+V / ⌘+V in the body.
In Outlook: Compose → Switch to HTML view → Paste.
The email will look beautifully formatted when the recipient opens it.
7
Come back and click "✅ Mark Sent & Track"
After sending, return to the dashboard and click this button. It records the send date and activates tracking so you can monitor opens and read time.
🎯
Pro tip from the dashboard: Paste into Gmail or Outlook in HTML mode. The read-time tracking pixels are automatically embedded — you'll see exactly how long each prospect spends reading your email.

Frequently Asked Questions
The things people ask most often
I opened the link and it says "No access token found" — what do I do?
Your unique link must include ?token= at the end. Check that you're using the full link as sent to you. If the link is broken or expired, contact your administrator to get a new one.
Can I change the company name on the email after generating it?
Yes — go to ⚙️ Settings, update your Brand Name, save, then click "🔄 Rebuild Email" again. The new email will use your updated name.
The AI generated the wrong toggles from my document — what can I do?
Click the ✏️ pencil icon on any toggle to edit its name and content manually. You can also delete a toggle and create a new one by clicking "+ Add toggle" and filling in the details yourself.
How does the read-time tracking work?
When you copy the email HTML and send it, a tiny invisible image is included in the email. When the recipient opens it and loads images, the tool records the time. This works in Gmail, Outlook, and most email clients that load images by default.
Can I use this for WhatsApp or SMS instead of email?
The tool is designed for email (HTML format). The generated text can be copied and manually adapted for other channels, but tracking only works via email.
I accidentally clicked "Reset all" on the tracker — is my data gone?
Yes — the tracker data resets. However, your companies, toggles, and brand settings are saved to the server and will still be there. Only the campaign tracking history (opened, read time, etc.) is cleared by the reset button. It's a good idea to click "📎 Export CSV" regularly to keep a backup.
How many toggles should I activate for each email?
2 to 4 is the sweet spot. Too many benefits in one email overwhelms the reader. Pick the ones most relevant to that specific company's industry and pain points.
Can multiple team members use the same account?
The token link is unique per user. If your whole team needs access, each person should have their own token. Contact your administrator to set this up.
